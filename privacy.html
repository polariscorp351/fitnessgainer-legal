Privacy Policy

Last Updated: [July 22, 2026]

1. Information We Collect

FitnessGainer allows you to create an account using Sign in with Apple or Google. When you create an account, we store your profile information, fitness goals, workout history, nutrition logs, streaks, badges, and subscription status securely using Supabase, our backend data provider, so that your information is available across your devices.

2. Friends and Shared Activity

If you choose to add friends within the app, your name, activity streak, and earned badges may be visible to the friends you connect with. You control who you send and accept friend requests from. No other personal data (such as your body stats, nutrition logs, or dietary restrictions) is shared with friends.

3. AI Features

If you use AI-powered features (such as meal suggestions, workout generation, or the AI Coach chat), the relevant information you provide is sent securely to our AI service provider to generate a response. This data is used solely to generate your requested content and is not used to train AI models or shared for any other purpose.

4. Third-Party Services

We use official Apple In-App Purchases to process subscription payments. Apple manages this payment data securely under their own privacy policy. We do not have access to your credit card information or banking details. We use Supabase for account authentication and data storage, and OpenAI (via a secured proxy) for AI-powered features.

5. Data Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time from the Profile screen. This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to how we collect, store, or use your data, this policy will be updated accordingly, and the "Last Updated" date above will reflect the most recent revision.
